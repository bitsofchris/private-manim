# The leaky bucket (quiz short, HyperFrames prototype)

## Concept
A stick figure pours a lecture into a bucket and feels full ("I get it").
Overnight the bucket leaks through holes nobody can see; the cold quiz the next
day is the dipstick that reads the real level (7/20) and shows where the holes
are, and the boring review schedule (day 1, day 4, week 2) patches them one at
a time.

- Teal = water = what was actually kept.
- Magenta = the one thing being pointed at in each beat (the feeling, then the
  score, then the leaks, then the current review date).
- Muted = construction (ground, the "felt full" line, puddles' outline).

Layout (1080x1920): props in the top band (y 270-520), figure left + bucket on
a crate right (y 540-1050), caption pill in the lower third (y ~1200-1400).
Everything inside the Shorts safe area (top 230, bottom 384, left 65, right 130).

## Storyboard (34 s, 30 fps, silent)

| # | Seconds | On screen | Caption |
|---|---------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | 0.0-4.0 | Figure beside a bucket. A lecture screen above pours a teal stream into the bucket; progress bar completes; water reaches the brim. Moving from frame 0. | Finished the lecture. |
| 2 | 4.0-8.0 | Screen gone. Figure throws arms up, hops. Magenta thought bubble: "I get it". Bucket full. | Feels like I know it. |
| 3 | 8.0-13.0 | Bubble gone. Moon and stars, figure dozes (z z). Three holes drip, puddles grow, water level sinks. | Overnight, it leaks. |
| 4 | 13.0-17.5 | Moon gone, quiz sheet with mostly crosses. Figure slumps, peers into the bucket. Dashed muted line where "full" was; magenta 7/20 at the real level. | Cold quiz: 7 out of 20. |
| 5a | 17.5-20.0 | Figure straightens up, frown goes. | Low score isn't failure. |
| 5b | 20.0-23.0 | 7/20 goes neutral; magenta rings pulse around the three holes; figure points at them. | It shows the leaks. |
| 6a | 23.0-25.5 | Quiz sheet replaced by three calendar chips. "day 1" lights magenta, first hole gets a patch, water rises. | Review after 1 day. |
| 6b | 25.5-28.0 | "day 1" turns teal, "day 4" magenta, second patch, water rises. | Again at 4 days. |
| 6c | 28.0-30.5 | "week 2" magenta, third patch, drips stop, water rises. | Again at 2 weeks. |
| 7 | 30.5-34.0 | All chips teal, figure arms up, bucket about 85% and holding. Motion stops at 33.0; last second is still. | Boring schedule. It holds. |

No title card, no outro, no call to action. No invented scores: after 7/20 the
level rises without numbers.
