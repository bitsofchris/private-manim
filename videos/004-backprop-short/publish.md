## Description

How a neural network actually learns, in 60 seconds: forward pass, loss, derivative, backprop, gradient descent.

We start with a single neuron (weights + bias, initialized randomly), run a forward pass to get a loss, then walk the computational graph backwards with the chain rule to get the gradient, and take a small step against it. That's one training step. Repeat.
