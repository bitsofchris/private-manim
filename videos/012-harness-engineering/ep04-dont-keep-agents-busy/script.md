## As recorded

The goal is not to keep agents busy. It's to maximize the rate at which you can understand things. A while back I made a video about how I use Obsidian to keep agents working for me. But the bottleneck has become me reviewing and understanding everything.

Now, sometimes it's useful to optimize for output, but other times you want to go slow so you can deliberately understand things. When I'm working through Karpathy's LLM lecture course, the agent writes the plumbing, but I write the core idea myself. That's the part I want to understand. So go fast on the parts that teach you nothing, but go slow on the core part that you're meant to struggle with.

Now, AI can't make you smarter on its own. You have to do that work. Keeping them busy is not the goal. Make sure you carve out time for your own growth. Block a slot on your day where you do the work, where you write something by hand, read it slowly, do the deep thinking, and are not just trying to keep agents busy.
