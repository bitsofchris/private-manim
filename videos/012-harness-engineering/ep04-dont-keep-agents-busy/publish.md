## Description

Is AI making us dumber? Only if you keep agents busy instead of growing how fast you understand.

Agents stream finished work at you until the pile is the bottleneck: you, reviewing and understanding all of it. So go fast on the parts that teach you nothing and slow on the core. Working through Karpathy's LLM course, the agent writes the plumbing (the training loop) and I write the core idea, attention, by hand.

Block a slot in your day to do the work yourself: write by hand, read slowly, think.
