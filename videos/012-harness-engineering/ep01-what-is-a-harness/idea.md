# Episode 1, What is an agent harness?: idea

**State:** raw idea, 2026-10-13. Not storyboarded. This image is the
hook, and the recurring set, of the harness engineering series
(`../SERIES.md`). The audience is engineers, so the "who is it for"
question below is settled: see the series page.

## The image (Chris's words, condensed)

Intelligence from a language model is a big blob of energy (intelligence,
tokens, whatever you call it) that you can put to use. How much use you get
depends on how much shape you give it:

1. **Poke it.** You get a blob back: a generic, regular-looking answer that
   does the thing in its blobby way.
2. **Prompt it, give it context.** It gains a little shape and texture, and
   the response gets more useful.
3. **Add memory and use.** A memory system (like OpenAugi), or simply
   using it more so it learns from you. It gets sharper.
4. **Harness engineering.** Now you aren't just layering on systems,
   tooling, and memory. The energy flows through tight, specific
   machinery, like trombone pipes intertwining or a maze of pipes, and it
   comes out as beautifully sharp work.

> "I don't know what the point of all this is, but this is the imagery I'm
> seeing … a kind of building your harness type thing."

## Visual notes

- The same blob carries through every stage, and only its container
  changes. That gives you continuity across the beats for free.
- Stages 1–3: the blob's output goes from a fuzzy spot to a textured
  shape to a sharp-edged one.
- Stage 4: the pipe maze. The energy flows through it as particles or a
  stream along curved, brass-like tubes, and comes out as a crisp output.
- Engine: probably HyperFrames, because flowing particles along SVG paths
  are cheap on canvas.
