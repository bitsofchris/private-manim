## As recorded

Recall is asking of all the positives my model classified, how... Sorry. Redo. Recall is asking of all the positives, how many did I actually catch? If there were 50 attacks and we only caught 40, recall's about 80%. We caught 40 out of the 50 that are possible. Now, precision is saying of the ones that I did predict were positive, how many actually were positive?

We flagged 100, 40 were attacks. Precision is 40%. Neither one uses the true negatives. In this case, there's almost 10,000 of them. That's why they still work when the attacks are rare. Recall is all about what you missed. These are the attacks that got through, and precision is about what you wasted. These are real users that you ended up blocking.
