## Description

How to read any AI architecture diagram: boxes, arrows, the residual path, and N×, in under a minute.

There's no formal standard, but every paper figure since Attention Is All You Need uses the same grammar. A box is a layer, an arrow is a tensor, flow is bottom to top, the arrow that bypasses a box is the residual stream, and N× means repeat. Shown on the real Figure 1 from the paper, redrawn one mark at a time.
