## Description

Cosine similarity vs dot product: cosine divides out both lengths, projection only one.

Two vectors, u = [1.5, 2] and v = [2, 0]. The dot product is 3: alignment with the lengths still in it. Cosine similarity divides by both lengths, 3 ÷ (2.5 × 2) = 0.6, pure direction between −1 and 1, which is what F.cosine_similarity gives you for comparing embeddings. Projection divides by only the target's length, 3 ÷ 2 = 1.5: the shadow u casts on v, which splits u into the part along v and the part left over. Even coordinates are projections, one onto each basis direction.
