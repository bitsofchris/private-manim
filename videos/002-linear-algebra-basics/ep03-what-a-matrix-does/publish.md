## Description

A matrix records where each input direction lands; every vector follows its recipe to the new place.

The matrix W sends e1 to (1, 0.5) and e2 to (−0.5, 1.25), one row each. The vector (3, 2) is a recipe: 3 of e1 plus 2 of e2. After the matrix, keep the recipe and use the landed directions: 3 of where e1 landed plus 2 of where e2 landed reaches (2, 4). In PyTorch that is y = x @ W, and W[0] is where e1 lands. Then a ring of vectors stretched along one direction and flattened onto it: amplify some directions, collapse others.
