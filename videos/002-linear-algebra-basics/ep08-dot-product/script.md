## As recorded

A dot product tells you how much two vectors point the same way. It's a way to measure similarity in AI models The way the math works is you multiply pairwise and then sum one, two, three dot four minus one, zero.

That's four minus two, zero, sum two. Watch the number as this arrow turns. Pointing the same direction is positive. At ninety degrees, they're perpendicular, orthogonal, exactly zero. No overlap at all. Pointing opposite is negative. That's why it's everywhere in AI. Embeddings that mean similar things point in similar directions, and the dot product is how you check.
