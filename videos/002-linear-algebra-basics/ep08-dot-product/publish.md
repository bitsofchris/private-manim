## Description

Dot product explained visually: positive means aligned, zero perpendicular, negative opposed.

Two arrows, a = [1, 1] and b turning around it. The dot product multiplies pairwise and sums: torch.dot(a, b) is 1·2 + 1·2 = 4 when they point the same way, 1·(−2) + 1·2 = 0 at ninety degrees, and −4 when they point opposite. Then why it is everywhere in AI: embeddings that mean similar things point in similar directions (king · queen = 5, king · banana = 0), and the same score is the core of attention (query · key).
