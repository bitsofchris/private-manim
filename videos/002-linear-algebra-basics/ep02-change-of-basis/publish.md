## Description

A vector's numbers are an address in a chosen basis: change the basis, same arrow, new address.

The point (3, 5, 2) in the usual x, y, z stays fixed while the grid shears around it to a new basis, and its address becomes (−2, 3, 2): −2 of v1, 3 of v2, 2 of v3, tip to tail. Then the interpretability angle: (1, 1) is a mix of x and y, but in a diagonal basis it's just (1, 0), one clean feature.
