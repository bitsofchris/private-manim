## As recorded

A vector is just a list of numbers, and it's the key to understanding the math behind AI. My son asked me, "What does northwest mean?" Now, northwest isn't a new direction. It's some west plus some north. That's just a vector, a move through space. Its numbers are the recipe for building that move out of directions we already agreed upon.

If the agreed directions are east and north, then minus two, two means two steps opposite east and two steps north. In code, a vector is just a list where order matters. Each position means something.

If we're describing a house, one dimension might be square feet, the other one price. Linear algebra is moving between those two views. The arrow lets you see it. The list lets you compute with it. Every, every embedding in AI is one of these, a list of numbers we treat as directions in space with thousands of dimensions.
