## Description

Linear algebra for AI engineers comes down to four ideas: vectors hold information, matrices reshape it.

A vector is a move: x = [3, 2] is 3 of e1 plus 2 of e2, a recipe of amounts along agreed directions. A matrix says where those directions land, and multiplying rebuilds the same recipe in the new directions: in PyTorch, x @ W weights each row of W by a number in x and adds them up, [2, 4]. A dot product measures alignment, and attention scores are dot products between queries and keys. In a neural network those matrices are learned, layer by layer: training finds which directions to amplify, ignore and mix.
