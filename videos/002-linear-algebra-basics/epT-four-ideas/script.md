## As recorded

You only need four ideas from linear algebra to understand what AI models are doing. a vector is a move. Its numbers are the recipe. Amounts along directions that we all agreed on. a matrix says where those directions land. Multiplying rebuilds that recipe in the new directions.

In PyTorch, x at w is just that. Each number in x weights a row of w and adds them up. a dot product measures alignment. Attention scores are dot products between queries and keys of your tokens. in a neural network, those matrices are learned layer by layer. Training finds which directions to amplify, which to ignore, and which ones to mix.

The model doesn't, the model doesn't understand in a human way. It learns moves that make the task easier. Vectors hold information. Matrices reshape it into the forms the model can use
